FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Email hello@lifttracker.ca.

Which iPhones does Lift Tracker work on?

Any iPhone running iOS 26 or later. Workout recaps and AI swap suggestions need an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence. Everything else, including import, works on every supported iPhone.

How much does Lift Tracker cost?

Lift Tracker is free to download, and a Lift Tracker Pro subscription unlocks the app. New subscribers get 14 days free. After that it's $3.99 a month or $39.99 a year in Canada. Outside Canada, the App Store shows your local price before you subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Lift Tracker. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or current period ends and you won't be charged again. You keep access until the period is over.

Do I need to make an account?

No. There's no sign up, no email and no password. Download the app and start lifting.

Where is my workout data stored?

On your iPhone. Lift Tracker doesn't send your workouts anywhere, and there's no server holding a copy. Because your data lives on your phone, it's included in your regular iPhone backup.

What happens when I get a new iPhone?

Set up your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, or transfer straight from your old one, and your workouts come with you. If you delete the app without a backup, your history can't be recovered.

Does it work with Apple Health and Apple Watch?

Lift Tracker saves your workouts, active calories and active minutes to Apple Health, with your permission. There isn't an Apple Watch app, but if you wear a Watch or another tracker that syncs heart rate to Apple Health, your live heart rate shows during cardio.

Can I track cardio and circuits too?

Yes. Outdoor runs and rides use GPS for your route, distance and pace. Treadmills, bikes, rowers and other machines log time and distance. Circuits handle timed or rep based exercises, rounds and rest.

Does importing a workout upload it anywhere?

No. Text, photos and files are read on your iPhone. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Apple's on device model turns them into a workout. On other iPhones, a built in parser does the job. Nothing is uploaded.

Can I use kilograms?

Yes. Switch between kg and lb anytime in your profile.

Is there an Android version?

No. Lift Tracker is made for iPhone.

How do I get help?

Email hello@lifttracker.ca or visit the Support page. We aim to reply within 1 to 2 business days.
