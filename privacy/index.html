Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Lift Tracker is made by Seth Technologies ("we", "our", or "us"), and we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our iOS application.

The short version

Lift Tracker has no accounts and no server holding your workouts. What you log is stored on your iPhone. We do not sell your data, and we do not use advertising networks, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the app's core functionality:

  • Workout data: exercises, sets, reps, weights, and session duration you log within the app. This data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.
  • Location data: only when you record an outdoor run or ride with GPS. Location is used solely to map your route and calculate distance and pace. Recording continues while your screen is locked until you end the session, and your route is saved with the workout on your device. We do not store or transmit your location data to any server.
  • Health data: if you grant permission, Lift Tracker saves your completed workouts, active calories, and distance to Apple Health. It reads your heart rate from Apple Health to show it live during cardio, and asks for permission to read your resting heart rate. Health data is never sent to us.
  • Text, photos, and files you choose: text you paste, photos you pick, and files you import are read on your device to build a workout or to read a cardio machine's display. This content is not uploaded to us.
  • Crash reports: Lift Tracker does not include analytics or tracking code. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. These cannot be used to identify you personally.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the app's functionality
  • To save your workout history locally on your device
  • To sync completed workouts to Apple Health, with your permission
  • To send notifications you have opted into, such as rest timer alerts, workout reminders, and weekly summaries

Processing on Your iPhone

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, workout import, exercise swap suggestions, and workout summaries use Apple's Foundation Models framework, which runs on your iPhone. Text in photos and files is recognised on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework. None of this content is sent to us.

Data Storage

Your workout data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. We do not operate a backend database of user workout data.

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, your Lift Tracker data is included in that backup. We have no access to your backups. If you delete the app, all locally stored data is permanently removed from your device.

Subscriptions and Payments

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. The app checks whether your subscription is active using Apple's StoreKit framework on your device. Apple provides us with standard sales reports that do not identify you.

Apple Services We Use

The app uses the following Apple frameworks and services:

  • Apple HealthKit: to save workouts and read heart rate, with your permission
  • Apple Core Location: to record GPS routes during outdoor runs and rides
  • Apple MapKit: to show your route on a map
  • Apple Photos and Vision: to read the photos and files you choose, on your device
  • Apple Foundation Models: for workout import, swap suggestions, and summaries on supported iPhones, on your device
  • Apple User Notifications: to deliver rest timer alerts and reminders, scheduled on your device
  • Apple StoreKit: to manage subscriptions and restore purchases

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use advertising networks or tracking SDKs.

Children's Privacy

Lift Tracker is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Your Rights

Since your data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it at all times. You can delete individual workouts within the app, or delete the app entirely to remove all associated data. You can change Health, location, and notification permissions at any time in your iPhone's Settings. For any privacy related requests, contact us at the address below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@lifttracker.ca.
